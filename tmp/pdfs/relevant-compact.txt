 47 (51–100 cases) indicates a higher risk of an outbreak and requires immediate attention. Critical Risk (more than 101 cases) indicates a serious situation that needs urgent intervention. These classifications help DengueWatch identify areas based on their risk levels, allowing health authorities to prioritize monitoring and take appropriate actions to prevent the spread of dengue. According to the National Clinical Guideline of Dengue for Timor-Leste, which follows World Health Organization standards, dengue cases are grouped using the age brackets < 1, 1-4, 5-14, 15-24, 25-49, 50-59, and 60+ instead of general population age groups, because dengue affects age groups differently. Infants under 1 year old still carry some protection from their mother's antibodies, while the 1-4 and 5-14 groups are the most affected, making up 33.5% and 37.8% of cases in Timor-Leste. The 15-24 group shows dengue spreading within communities, the 25-49 group covers working adults exposed through daily activities, and the 50-59 and 60+ groups are separated since older adults face higher risk of severe illness or death. Using this age grouping helps the DengueWatch system and its Random Forest model make more accurate predictions of dengue hotspots in Iligan City [36]. Forecast Target and Model Specification 

===PAGE 52===
 50 1. Forecast Horizon, Geographic Unit, and Target - DengueWatch produces a one-calendar-month-ahead forecast for each of Iligan City's 44 barangays. The prediction unit is one barangay for one target calendar month. For each barangay, the model estimates the number of CHO-confirmed dengue cases expected in month t+1, using only information available by the forecast cutoff in month t. Formal Target Statement - Using CHO-confirmed case records and other permitted information available through the forecast cutoff in month t, the system estimates the number of confirmed dengue cases in each barangay during the following calendar month, t+1. A barangay is flagged as a potential planning hotspot when its estimated count reaches at least 6 cases. Forecast Specification Element Operational Definition Time unit and horizon Calendar-month observations; forecast the next calendar month (one month ahead). Geographic unit Each of the 44 official barangays in Iligan City, with one barangay-month forecast row. Primary model target Count of CHO-confirmed dengue case records assigned to the target month and barangay, using the project's approved case-date definition. Hotspot flag Potential planning hotspot if the estimated next-month count is 6 or more. This is an internal 

===PAGE 53===
 51 planning threshold, not an official DOH outbreak definition Forecast origin A documented issue date and cutoff. The primary model evaluation uses completed input months. Operational month-to-date updates, if displayed, are marked provisional and use only records confirmed by that cutoff. Permitted Information and Interpretation - Predictors may include lagged CHO-confirmed case counts, barangay identifiers or stable geographic characteristics, and weather observations available by the issue cutoff. Weather values observed after the cutoff and dengue cases from the target month are excluded from model inputs. Pending reports are not treated as confirmed cases; if the operational display is updated during an incomplete month, it is explicitly labeled provisional. 2. Training, Validation, and Reporting - To demonstrate future forecasting rather than reproduction of a same-month risk label, the target for every training example is the confirmed case count in the subsequent month. Features are constructed as they would have been known at the forecast issue date. No case count, weather observation, or derived statistic from the target month may enter that month's predictors.
