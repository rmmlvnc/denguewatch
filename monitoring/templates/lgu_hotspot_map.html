Map Information

This is a historical case map. It combines dengue records from the loaded datasets with CHO-confirmed reports entered in DengueWatch. Records are grouped by reported barangay and, when available, sitio, purok, or street name. Counts represent source records, not necessarily unique people.

Official barangay boundaries show where recorded cases are distributed. Select a barangay to view its reported sitio / purok / street details. Records without an official Iligan barangay match are not shown on the map.

Use the year and risk filters to explore the selected historical records. Boundary colors reflect recorded case volume for that selection, not AI forecasts. Risk labels use DengueWatch dashboard thresholds; they are not official DOH classifications or individual risk assessments.
